[size=5][b]Ceifadoras — Scythes for Valheim[/b][/size]

Four two-handed scythes, one for each stage of the game, with their own models, icons and stats.

[b]The scythes[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Iron Reaper[/b] — Forge level 3 (Ancient Bark, Iron, Troll Hide). 70 slash.
[*][b]Black Metal Reaper[/b] — Forge level 4 (same recipe as the Black Metal Battleaxe). 105 slash.
[*][b]Obsidian Reaper[/b] — Black Forge level 2 (Black Metal, Eitr, Yggdrasil Wood, Obsidian, Blood Clots). 120 slash + [color=#cc3333]bleeding[/color].
[*][b]Flametal Reaper[/b] — Black Forge level 3 (Flametal, Ashwood, Charred Bone, Asksvin Hide). 135 slash + [color=#66cc44]poison[/color].
[/list]

[b]Combat[/b]
[list]
[*]4-hit combo: two battleaxe swings, then two spinning slashes that hit everyone around you.
[*]Special attack: atgeir-style twirl.
[*]One hit per enemy per swing.
[*]Stamina matches the vanilla battleaxe (attacks) and atgeir (special) of the same tier.
[*]Battleaxe sounds, screen shake on hit and parry, and the battleaxe weapon trail.
[/list]

[b]Styles[/b]
Like shields, each scythe can be crafted with the look of the earlier scythes — pick the style at the workbench.
[list]
[*]Iron: its own look
[*]Black Metal: + Iron
[*]Obsidian: + Iron and Black Metal
[*]Flametal: all four
[/list]
The style only changes the look; damage and effects stay those of the scythe you crafted.

[b]Requirements[/b]
[list]
[*]BepInExPack Valheim
[*]Jotunn
[*][b]Every player on the server needs the mod.[/b]
[/list]

[b]Installation[/b]
Extract the [i]BepInEx[/i] folder from the zip into your Valheim folder (Steam > Valheim > Manage > Browse local files).
It should end up as: Valheim\BepInEx\plugins\CeifadoraObsidiana\CeifadoraObsidiana.dll

[b]Configuration[/b]
BepInEx\config\fabiano.ceifadoraobsidiana.cfg (created on first launch). Damage and stamina per scythe, sounds, trail, styles, pixel textures and how the scythe sits in the hands. Many settings apply while the game is running — just save the file.
